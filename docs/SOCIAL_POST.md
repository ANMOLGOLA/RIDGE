# Ready-to-Post Social Media Draft

Every monsoon, mountain highways across India's North-Eastern Region like NH-10 and NH-6 are cut off by sudden landslides and flash floods, stranding medicine, food, and freight for days. Standard navigation apps remain completely blind to these regional advisories.

We built **RIDGE** (Regional Intelligence & Geospatial Efficiency)—an AI-powered road intelligence platform that solves this. 

RIDGE uses **TinyFish** to autonomously discover, fetch, and extract live road closure bulletins from local news, district police alerts, and government blogs. It scores geomorphological disruption risks with XGBoost, dynamically penalties hazardous segments on OpenStreetMap, and computes safer alternative routes in real time.

We’re also pioneering Dashcam-as-a-Service: subsidized 4K AI dashcams stream privacy-blurred road hazard detections from moving trucks back into the safety network.

Smarter logistics. Safer routes. A stronger North-East.
